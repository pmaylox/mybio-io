"Far out in the uncharted backwaters of the unfashionable end of the western spiral arm of the Galaxy lies a small unregarded yellow sun. Orbiting this at a distance of roughly ninety-two million miles is an utterly insignificant little blue green planet whose ape-descended life forms are so amazingly primitive that they still think digital watches are a pretty neat idea."1.

I am a simple middle-aged man living in the best state in the Union, in my opinion, Oklahoma. Now I wasn't always an Okie. I am military brat by upbringing. Born in Mesa, AZ I moved to, in order, San Antonio, TX; Panama City, FL; New Portsmouth, NH; Plattsburg, NY; Waikiki, HI; Cape Girardeau, Mo; Redlands, CA; and Norman, OK

But then I grew up

After a disastrous attempt at college, I met, married and procreated. It was at this time with no life trajectory prospects, I myself joined the Air Force and became a Security Specialist, which is fancy talk for security guard. I seved at Great Falls, MT; and anchorage, AK. Tragically I was witness to a massive plane crash that left me emotionally scarred.

Now I am happily back in Oklahoma and HAPPILY married and have 6 count them 6 Grandchildren!! I work for the State of Oklahoma. I can retire in 5 more laborious years.
