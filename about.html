Welcome to my story

"Far out in the uncharted backwaters of the unfashionable end of the western spiral arm of the Galaxy lies a small unregarded yellow sun. Orbiting this at a distance of roughly ninety-two million miles is an utterly insignificant little blue-green planet whose ape-descended life forms are so amazingly primitive that they still think digital watches are a pretty neat idea."1.

I am a lifelong learner, a proud son of the military, and a resident of Oklahoma who has built a life grounded in service, curiosity, and resilience. My journey has taken me across the country and around the world, and each chapter has shaped the perspective I bring to today.

I was born in Mesa, Arizona, and grew up moving from place to place as a military brat. My early years took me through Panama City, Florida; New Portsmouth, New Hampshire; Plattsburg, New York; Waikiki, Hawaii; Cape Girardeau, Missouri; Redlands, California; and finally Norman, Oklahoma. Each move taught me adaptability, perspective, and the value of meeting new people with an open mind. Today, I carry that experience with me in both my personal and professional life.
